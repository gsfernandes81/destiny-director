Autopost Settings

Toggle whether anchor produces each feed, which channel each posts to, and general bot settings (accent colours, the alerts/log channels, and the rest). These are global, bot-wide switches — they don't change which servers follow a feed. Most changes apply on the next scheduled post; a few — mainly a feed's very first channel (going from unconfigured to configured) — only take effect after a bot restart, since that also decides whether the feed's cron/command registers at all.
